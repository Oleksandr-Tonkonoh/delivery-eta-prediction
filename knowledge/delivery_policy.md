# Delivery Policy

## Standard delivery

Standard delivery is available for eligible orders.
The estimated delivery time depends on the delivery model's prediction
and may change because of traffic, weather, restaurant preparation,
and courier availability.

## Delivery delays

If an order is delayed, the customer should check the latest order status.
The estimated delivery time is an estimate, not a guaranteed arrival time.

## Order status

The customer should use the order tracking page to check the latest status.
If the status has not changed for an extended period, the customer should
contact customer support.
