# Courier FAQ

## How can I track an order?

Customers can use the order tracking page to see the latest available status.

## Why is an order late?

Possible reasons include traffic, bad weather, restaurant preparation time,
and courier availability.

## What should I do if the order status does not change?

The customer should contact customer support and provide the order reference.

## Is the estimated delivery time guaranteed?

No. The estimated delivery time is a prediction and may change.
