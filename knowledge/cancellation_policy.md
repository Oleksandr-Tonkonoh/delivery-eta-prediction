# Cancellation Policy

## Cancellation requests

Cancellation availability depends on the current order status.
Customers should check the cancellation option displayed in the application.

## Orders already being prepared

An order that is already being prepared may not be eligible for cancellation.
The customer should contact customer support if the cancellation option
is unavailable.

## Refunds

Refund eligibility and processing time depend on the circumstances
and the applicable order policy.
Customers should contact customer support for a decision about a specific order.
